1. Product Management
	•	Defines product vision and roadmap.
	•	Prioritizes features based on user needs, feedback, and business goals.
	•	Coordinates between design, engineering, and marketing.

2. User Research & Customer Development
	•	Understands customer pain points and validates product-market fit.
	•	Conducts interviews, surveys, usability testing, and early user feedback.

3. Marketing & Growth
	•	Builds brand awareness and demand generation.
	•	Runs acquisition campaigns, SEO/SEM, content marketing, social media, etc.
	•	Often includes growth hacking and experimentation.

4. Customer Success / Support
	•	Ensures customers get value from the product.
	•	Handles onboarding, education, and ongoing support.
	•	Drives retention, satisfaction, and referrals.

5. Operations
	•	Manages day-to-day logistics and processes.
	•	Covers vendor relationships, legal compliance, facilities, and sometimes HR.
	•	Ensures the company runs smoothly as it scales.

6. Finance & Accounting
	•	Manages budgeting, forecasting, cash flow, and financial reporting.
	•	Handles taxes, payroll, and regulatory compliance.

7. Legal & Compliance
	•	Drafts and reviews contracts, terms of service, privacy policies.
	•	Ensures IP protection (e.g., patents, trademarks).
	•	Navigates regulatory frameworks (especially in fintech, healthtech, etc.).

8. Human Resources & Talent
	•	Recruits, hires, and retains talent.
	•	Manages compensation, benefits, team culture, and performance reviews.

9. Design (UX/UI)
	•	Shapes user experience and product usability.
	•	Includes both visual design and interaction design.

10. Data & Analytics
	•	Tracks KPIs and performance metrics.
	•	Supports product and business decisions with insights and dashboards.
